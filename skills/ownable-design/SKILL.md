# Ownable

**Art direction that makes AI design hard to logo-swap.**

Ownable is a platform-agnostic art-direction skill. It does not prescribe a fashionable UI style. It helps an agent discover, choose, build, and verify a visual world that feels specific to the actual subject: brand, product, person, place, culture, material, story, behavior, and proof.

Ownable is not a generic frontend craft handbook. It composes with specialist skills rather than shadow-copying them.

## Operating contract

The explicit brief, verified product/brand truth, required content and functionality, legal/safety requirements, accessibility needs, incumbent product commitments, and native platform constraints are binding.

For requests to create, build, redesign, improve, fix, polish, transform, or otherwise modify an experience, Ownable is **action-first**. Diagnosis chooses the intervention; the changed artifact is the deliverable when the environment can safely perform the work. A critique-only answer is appropriate when the user explicitly asks for critique/analysis, the environment is read-only, or scope/authorization prevents modification.

Never pretend that an asset was generated, a background was removed, a motion was fixed, a source was verified, or a visual result was inspected when it was not.

### Companion precedence

When loaded and applicable, these companions outrank Ownable inside their specialist domains:

- **Taste Skill** — frontend taste, anti-slop, composition guidance, design dials, and its scoped visual heuristics.
- **Emil Kowalski skills** — motion/animation mechanics and craft: timing, easing, springs, gestures, interruptibility, transform origins, reduced-motion behavior, and interaction feel.
- **Impeccable** — product/design-world methodology, refinement vs redesign, design systems, UX/states, accessibility, responsive/technical discipline, implementation craft, progressive enhancement, rendered verification, and QA.

Do not duplicate their detailed rules here. Ownable owns **subject specificity, visual-language discovery, concept fit, medium choice, ownability, art-direction intervention, and whole-experience coherence**.

When Impeccable is active for a new or replacement visual world, use its current new-work/direction-selection workflow; Ownable supplies the subject evidence, reference universe, anti-default checks, and ownability lens. When Impeccable is active for refinement/polish, use its current scope and verification workflow rather than inventing a competing loop.

## Workflow

Use five phases:

1. **Ground** — learn what is true and distinctive.
2. **Discover** — explore and choose the visual world.
3. **Architect** — shape content, structure, sequence, and representation.
4. **Produce** — execute the justified interventions with available capabilities.
5. **Verify** — inspect the rendered experience and finish the highest-impact problems.

Read [reference/direction-discovery.md](reference/direction-discovery.md) when the visual direction is open, generic, being replaced, or needs broader reference discovery.

Read [reference/intervention-playbook.md](reference/intervention-playbook.md) when actively building, redesigning, fixing, or transforming an experience.

## Phase 1 — Ground

### 1. Brief, truth, and required behavior bind
Ownable shapes art direction inside the real product and brief. It does not use creativity as permission to erase required content, routes, deep links, legal/safety material, SEO/navigation obligations, product semantics, native affordances, or factual claims.

### 2. Mine the subject before the moodboard
Inspect what is already unique: product/service, people, place, history, materials, architecture, packaging, environment, language, process, behavior, photography, artifacts, culture, data, and verified proof. The subject should provide the first design vocabulary.

### 3. Evidence before aesthetics; constraints can become authorship
Important art-direction decisions should trace to something real or intentionally conceptual. “Looks premium” is not enough. Limited imagery, regulation, dense information, localization, legacy content, unusual materials, or platform constraints can become part of the direction instead of being hidden by generic decoration.

### 4. Brand before category; culture without costume
Category conventions are context, not identity. Do not reduce jewellery to gold, music to waveforms, healthcare to crosses, AI to neon grids, or luxury to black-and-white minimalism.

For culturally rooted work, draw from relevant materials, language, rituals, architecture, craft, landscapes, objects, archives, visual history, and verified context. Do not invent significance or use sacred/identity-bearing motifs as decorative novelty.

### 5. Define a Creative North Star and use it as a filter
For substantial work, define one concise project-specific thesis: an idea, material, story, transformation, spatial reference, cultural cue, physical behavior, or visual metaphor that explains why the experience belongs to this subject.

The North Star should change decisions without becoming wallpaper. Extract principles from it instead of stamping a literal motif onto every section.

### 6. Build distributed identity and run the logo-swap test
Choose a small set of subject-derived identity carriers: for example material treatment, crop logic, framing, type gesture, spatial rule, image behavior, information pattern, or interaction principle.

Distribute them with variation beyond the logo and hero. If swapping only logo/copy makes the major visual language equally plausible for an unrelated competitor, revisit the concept, references, assets, or system.

## Phase 2 — Discover

### 7. Style is vocabulary, not identity
“Glassmorphism,” “bento,” “minimal,” “editorial,” “neo-brutal,” “skeuomorphic,” “futuristic,” “retro,” and similar labels may describe one or more execution characteristics. None is a Creative North Star by itself.

Do not choose a named style first and force the subject into it. A strong direction may have no useful style label at all.

### 8. Search the reference universe, not a trend catalog
When direction is meaningfully open and reference access exists, research broadly: the subject itself, relevant competitors, high-craft digital work, the audience's cultural world, and adjacent disciplines such as editorial, print, packaging, architecture, interiors, industrial design, fashion, photography, film titles, signage, exhibitions, maps, archives, scientific/technical graphics, and physical materials.

Search for the subject's objects, processes, places, behaviors, and evidence — not only “best [category] website” or a style keyword.

### 9. Decompose visual language into dimensions
Do not treat UI style as one switch. Consider composition/spatial grammar, typography, color/light, surface/material, geometry/chrome, imagery/illustration, density/rhythm, information representation, motion/interaction, and responsive transformation independently.

A project may use a bento-like information structure without a bento-looking material system, or restrained glass surfaces inside an otherwise editorial world. Each dimension must still belong to one coherent direction.

### 10. Explore materially different candidate worlds when the direction is open
Do not settle on the first plausible aesthetic. Consider genuinely different visual systems, not recolors of one layout.

When Impeccable is active, use its current direction-selection workflow. Without that companion, derive several grounded candidates, discard near-duplicates, compare the strongest ones against the subject and constraints, and commit to the best fit. Do not burden the user with options unless approval is required or requested.

### 11. Transform references at the principle level
Reference sources are raw material, not finished identity. Prefer extracting hierarchy, tension, rhythm, framing, material logic, information strategy, spatial behavior, or interaction principles over copying recognizable surface styling.

The deeper the useful abstraction, the less likely the result becomes a clone.

### 12. Choose by fit, not fashion or familiarity
A direction should earn its place through subject resonance, audience/use-context fit, content behavior, product/task clarity, proof/truth needs, brand commitments, technical feasibility, longevity, and ownability.

Do not let model familiarity masquerade as fit. A fashionable glass, bento, editorial, brutalist, cinematic, or minimal treatment loses when a less fashionable direction better serves the subject.

### 13. Hybridize only through one coherent world
Combining visual languages is allowed when each borrowed principle serves the same North Star. Reject Frankenstyles assembled from unrelated trendy ingredients.

If the combination can only be explained by listing style names, rather than by explaining the subject and its visual logic, it is not resolved.

## Phase 3 — Architect

### 14. Let content shape composition
Do not choose a familiar shell first and force content into it. Copy length, image role, evidence, product behavior, hierarchy, task, and narrative should determine composition.

Similar structures are useful when content genuinely has the same job. Repeated structure by convenience is template behavior.

### 15. Every section, page, route, and major screen earns its place
A structural unit should add information, proof, task value, narrative progression, atmosphere, interaction, orientation, or conversion support.

Do not add a block because templates usually contain it, because the page feels short, or because an unused asset exists. Required units from the brief remain protected.

### 16. Use structural surgery when structure is the problem
Within scope, Ownable may sort, reorder, regroup, relabel, merge, split, replace, promote, demote, or remove navigation items, tabs, sections, and content groups when that improves hierarchy, comprehension, proof sequencing, scanning, conversion, or task priority.

If two optional sections do the same job, merge, differentiate, or remove. If a section concept is wrong, replace the section rather than polishing the wrong shell.

### 17. Design the whole experience as a sequence or flow
For narrative/editorial/marketing work, shape opening, orientation, proof, depth, contrast, quieter passages, interaction, decision/action, and ending. Not every section should carry equal visual intensity.

For task-oriented products, apply the principle to task priority, state progression, continuity, and recovery rather than forcing storytelling onto the interface.

### 18. Choose the representation that communicates best
Do not assume every idea should become prose, every proof point a card, every process a paragraph, every comparison a table, or every story an image.

Choose among editorial text, annotated image, diagram, timeline, map, comparison, chart, process graphic, interactive explainer, video, 3D/spatial view, audio, or deliberate absence of media based on what communicates most clearly and meaningfully.

Information graphics must represent real information. Never invent data, precision, geography, causality, or relationships for visual sophistication.

### 19. Re-art-direct across contexts instead of merely resizing
Desktop, tablet, mobile, touch, reduced-capability, and other supported contexts do not have to share the exact same crop, media, hierarchy, sequence, interaction, detail level, or signature moment.

Adapt creatively while preserving identity, truth, required content, and functionality. Impeccable remains authoritative for technical responsive behavior when active.

### 20. Visual economy is not minimalism; consistency is not repetition
Sparse is not automatically premium and rich is not automatically cluttered. Every visible element and every large empty area consumes attention.

Consistency comes from shared rules, not from repeating the same card grid, crop, hero, photograph, motif, effect, or animation everywhere. Reuse quality standards and implementation primitives; avoid reusing an unrelated project's recognizable surface fingerprint.

## Phase 4 — Produce

### 21. Modification means execute the fix
For active design work, audit is diagnosis. Use the available environment to make justified changes rather than returning a recommendation list.

Do not offload transparent cutouts, better crops, asset sourcing, generated illustration, section restructuring, or other work to the user when the environment can safely perform it.

### 22. Treat every expressive medium as editable art-direction material
Typography, color/material, content framing, imagery, illustration, iconography, graphic marks, information graphics, data visualization, motion, interaction, video, 3D/spatial media, sound, backgrounds/environmental layers, proof presentation, and responsive treatment can all be kept, refined, transformed, replaced, added, simplified, regrouped, or removed within scope.

Every intervention should answer: **why this medium, why here, and why this treatment?**

### 23. Treat assets as a source pool, not a checklist
Weak supplied media is not sacred. For an underperforming asset, choose the least invasive solution that actually works: keep, reframe, transform, replace with a stronger real asset, generate a better asset, or remove it.

Avoid exact and perceptual repetition unless reuse has a real role. Keep primary campaign media privileged unless its return is a deliberate callback or materially different use.

### 24. Generate and transform assets when the concept earns it
When supported, proactively create or transform the missing visual rather than weakening a strong concept.

Valid interventions include truthful cleanup, alpha cutouts/background removal, masking, relighting, reframing, perspective correction, object removal, background work, compositing, upscale/detail recovery, responsive crops/variants, and generated photographic or non-photographic assets.

Authored media may include watercolor, gouache, pencil, charcoal, ink, engraving, collage, textured editorial work, diagrammatic composition, mixed media, stylized 3D, and other subject-appropriate languages. Generated media is not automatically premium and must not become filler.

### 25. Proof, truth, and provenance outrank decorative prestige
When reassurance matters, prefer truthful evidence: real work, products, facilities, people, process, credentials, locations, reviews, or other verified proof.

For claim-sensitive people/products/places/facilities/events/medical subjects or other decision-bearing moments, verified real imagery outranks generated substitution. Web visibility does not imply reuse rights; prefer official, owned, licensed, public-domain, or otherwise permissioned production sources.

Logo cleanup is not logo redesign. Enhancement must not invent misleading features, outcomes, materials, facilities, credentials, or characteristics.

When celebrities, ambassadors, founders, doctors, models, or performers appear, preserve the gravity of the underlying brand/product unless that person is the primary brand.

### 26. Use motion and interaction as art-direction material, not garnish
Motion can carry identity, hierarchy, continuity, explanation, narrative progression, state change, feedback, or a rare signature moment. Do not animate because a premium site is expected to move.

Ownable decides whether motion belongs and what conceptual role it serves. When Emil Kowalski skills are active, defer timing, easing, springs, gestures, interruptibility, transform origins, reduced motion, and related implementation craft to them.

### 27. Explore advanced media broadly; ship only what the concept earns
3D, WebGL, shaders, generative graphics, cinematic video, image sequences, physics, spatial galleries, sound, unusual navigation, configurators, and similar techniques may be considered proactively.

They survive only when they improve the concept, communication, task, or experience. Technology availability is not justification.

## Phase 5 — Verify

### 28. Require visual evidence for visual conclusions
Code, tokens, and implementation intent are not proof that a visual experience works.

When rendering, preview, screenshot, simulator, browser, or comparable evidence is available, inspect the actual result before making strong claims about composition, crop, hierarchy, polish, responsiveness, atmosphere, or impact.

For substantial web work, representative desktop and mobile viewing is the normal minimum when both are in scope. When Impeccable is active, use its bounded verification workflow instead of creating a second one.

### 29. Fix the highest-impact experience first
Do not perfect tiny spacing, shadows, borders, or hover polish while the concept, first viewport, major structure, primary media, proof, hierarchy, section sequence, or task path is still weak.

Fix causes in descending impact, then use specialist skills for detailed craft.

### 30. Run a first-time visitor-eye finish pass
Inspect the built experience as a visitor, not as its implementer. Judge opening impression, focal point, clarity, curiosity, credibility, emotional tone, pacing, repetition, dead/awkward areas, asset quality, section transitions, moments of delight, action clarity, and whether anything feels templated, arbitrary, cheap, synthetic in the wrong way, or unfinished.

Fix the remaining high-impact issues in a bounded pass, then stop.

### 31. Run the final ownability gate
Before considering substantial art direction complete, verify that:

- the work feels specific to its subject rather than merely category-correct;
- the North Star is visible in decisions without becoming a repeated gimmick;
- identity carriers survive beyond the logo and opening viewport;
- the selected visual language came from fit rather than a named-style shortcut;
- references were transformed rather than cloned;
- composition follows content and each major structural unit earns its place;
- medium choices communicate rather than decorate;
- justified fixes the environment could perform were actually executed;
- assets are truthful, purposeful, coherent, and not needlessly repetitive;
- motion/advanced media exists only where earned;
- responsive contexts were re-art-directed when necessary rather than merely shrunk;
- rendered evidence supports strong visual-quality claims when such evidence is available;
- the visitor-eye pass finds no unresolved high-impact weakness;
- the major surface decisions could not be transplanted unchanged to an unrelated project without losing meaning.

## Keep Ownable narrow

Do not add generic cold-audit commands, price tiers, framework-specific recipes, platform-specific workflow rules, arbitrary style catalogs, or duplicate motion/accessibility/performance/state/QA systems.

When proposing a new rule, first ask whether it belongs in an existing rule, a conditional reference file, or a companion specialist skill.
